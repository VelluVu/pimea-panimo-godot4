PIMEA PANIMO — BEER STYLE REFERENCE
Compiled after the 2026-09-14 realism/duplicate audit against real-world
(BJCP-style) brewing guidelines, scaled for this game's simplified formulas.

HOW THE GAME COMPUTES A BREW (src/brewing/brew_resolver.gd)
  final_ebc = round(weighted_ebc_sum / total_malt_weight_kg)
  final_ibu = round(total_alpha_acids / 10), where
              total_alpha_acids = sum(hop.alpha_acids * grams_used)
  A style matches when ALL of these hold:
    - yeast on the table == required_yeast_id
    - required_malt_id (if set, -1 = none) is present among the malts used
    - total malt weight >= min_malt_weight
    - final_ebc is inside [min_ebc, max_ebc]
    - final_ibu is inside [min_ibu, max_ibu]
  Matching is first-match-wins. Styles with a required_malt_id are checked
  BEFORE malt-agnostic styles (see _prioritize_required_malt_styles()), so a
  style defined by a specific malt always gets first look over a generic
  catch-all like Pale Ale — this is what keeps Hefeweizen/Witbier/Saison from
  being swallowed by wider ale ranges that happen to overlap them.

===============================================================================
STYLE-BY-STYLE
===============================================================================

Each entry: game values, then the real-world reference it was checked
against and any note on what changed / why it's scaled the way it is.

--- Kotikalja (home-brewed "small beer", not a BJCP style) -------------------
  yeast 300 (Kotikalja) | EBC <=4 | ABV 2.8%
  Traditional Finnish low-alcohol table drink, not a real brewing style —
  intentionally the trivial always-available starter recipe. No change.

--- Bulkkilager (mass-market macro lager) -------------------------------------
  yeast 301 (default) | EBC 1-10 | IBU 8-20 | ABV 4.5%
  Real: American Light/Standard Lager, SRM 2-3.5 (~EBC 4-7), IBU 8-18.
  CHANGED: max_ibu 40 -> 20 (was unrealistically bitter for a macro lager).

--- Tumma Lager (dark lager, Dunkel/Schwarzbier) ------------------------------
  yeast 301 (default) | EBC 28-70 | IBU 8-34 | ABV 4.8%
  Real: Munich Dunkel SRM 14-28 (~EBC 28-55), IBU 18-28. Left as-is —
  window sits close enough to real and is needed as the "generic dark
  lager" catch-all; disambiguated from Doppelbock by malt weight, from
  Baltic Portteri by IBU (no overlap: 34 vs 35).

--- Pale Ale (American Pale Ale) ----------------------------------------------
  yeast 302 (Ale) | EBC 4-30 | IBU 12-40 | ABV 5.0%
  Real: SRM 5-10 (~EBC 10-20), IBU 30-50. min_ibu deliberately compressed
  to 12 (real IBU 30 needs unrealistic hop amounts under this game's
  formula) — this was the original reported bug: 3kg pale-ale malt + 8g
  Cascade + 8g Centennial (IBU 13) was misresolving as Hefeweizen before
  the fix. Verified live: now resolves to Pale Ale at 103% quality.

--- Amber Ale (American Amber Ale) --------------------------------------------
  yeast 302 (Ale) | EBC 20-40 | IBU 25-40 | ABV 5.5%
  Real: SRM 10-17 (~EBC 20-33), IBU 25-40. Already closely matches.

--- IPA (American IPA) ---------------------------------------------------------
  yeast 302 (Ale) | min 4kg malt | EBC 12-28 | IBU 40-200 | ABV 6.5%
  Real: SRM 6-14 (~EBC 12-28), IBU 40-70. EBC matches almost exactly; IBU
  ceiling left generously high (200) since the compressed formula makes
  very high IBU hard to reach with sane hop amounts anyway.

--- Vienna Lager -----------------------------------------------------------
  yeast 301 (default) | EBC 15-19 | IBU 18-26 | ABV 5.2%
  Real: SRM 7-14 (~EBC 14-28), IBU 18-30. Game window sits inside the real
  range, fine.

--- Helles (Munich Helles) ------------------------------------------------
  yeast 301 (default) | EBC 11-14 | IBU 14-20 | ABV 4.9%
  Real: SRM 3-5 (~EBC 6-10), IBU 16-22 — real Helles is paler than the
  current game window. Left as-is: raising it would collide with Bulkki-
  lager's EBC ceiling (10) and it's already disambiguated fine from
  Alkoholiton Lager (identical EBC window, different required yeast).

--- Märzen / Oktoberfest ----------------------------------------------------
  yeast 301 (default) | EBC 20-27 | IBU 20-28 | ABV 5.8%
  Real: SRM 7-14 (~EBC 14-28), IBU 18-24. Reasonably matches.

--- Baltic Portteri (Baltic Porter) -----------------------------------------
  yeast 301 (default) | min 4kg malt | EBC 28-70 | IBU 35-48 | ABV 7.5%
  Real: SRM 17-30 (~EBC 34-59), IBU 20-40. Broadly fine; IBU floor is a
  touch high vs real but keeps a clean gap from Tumma Lager.

--- Doppelbock ----------------------------------------------------------------
  yeast 301 (default) | min 6kg malt | EBC 20-50 | IBU 16-26 | ABV 8.0%
  Real: SRM 6-25 (~EBC 12-49), IBU 16-26.
  CHANGED (was clearly wrong): EBC was 71-95 — that's Imperial Stout
  territory, far darker than any real Doppelbock. Corrected to 20-50 to
  match reality. min_malt_weight raised 4 -> 6 kg: realistic (a
  Doppelbock's 7-10% ABV genuinely needs a much bigger grain bill than a
  Dunkel's 4.5-5.6%) and it doubles as the disambiguator against Märzen's
  overlapping EBC/IBU window — a big-enough batch reads as Doppelbock, a
  normal-sized one stays Märzen/Tumma Lager.

--- Hefeweizen ------------------------------------------------------------
  yeast 302 (Ale) | required malt: Vehnämallas (103, wheat)
  EBC 4-8 | IBU 8-15 | ABV 5.2%
  Real: SRM 2-6 (~EBC 4-12), IBU 8-15. Matches well.
  CHANGED (original bug fix): added required_malt_id so a wheat beer must
  actually contain wheat malt — previously any malt landing in the right
  EBC/IBU window could produce "Hefeweizen," which is how 3kg pale-ale
  malt + hops was misresolving.

--- Witbier -----------------------------------------------------------------
  yeast 302 (Ale) | required malt: Vehnämallas (103, wheat)
  EBC 9-13 | IBU 12-18 | ABV 4.8%
  Real: SRM 2-4 (~EBC 4-8), IBU 10-20 — real Witbier is a touch paler than
  the game window, but 9-13 was kept because dropping it to 4-8 would
  collide with Hefeweizen's identical wheat requirement and EBC 4-8 window.
  Also required_malt_id added (same reasoning as Hefeweizen).
  BUG FOUND & FIXED: Witbier's own minimum-ingredient combo was resolving
  to "Pale Ale" (Pale Ale's wide EBC 4-30/IBU 12-40 window with no malt
  requirement caught it first, since "pale_ale.tres" sorts alphabetically
  before "witbier.tres"). Fixed via the required-malt-priority resolver
  change described above. Verified: now resolves to Witbier correctly.

--- Session Ale (English Ordinary/Session Bitter analog) --------------------
  yeast 302 (Ale) | required malt: Karamelli mallas (102, caramell)
  EBC 6-12 | IBU 10-20 | ABV 3.8%
  Real: SRM 4-14 (~EBC 8-28), IBU 25-35.
  CHANGED: was EBC 1-3 — unrealistically pale even for a session beer.
  Real English Ordinary/Session Bitter commonly includes a portion of
  crystal/caramel malt for color and character, so required_malt_id =
  Karamelli mallas is a realistic, defensible fix (same reasoning as
  Hefeweizen/Witbier's wheat requirement). EBC raised to 6-12 — closer to
  real without reaching the full SRM14 (~EBC28) ceiling, which would
  collide with Amber Ale's window (20-40). Because it now has a
  required_malt_id, it's protected from the Pale Ale collision class by
  the same _prioritize_required_malt_styles() resolver fix as Witbier/
  Saison. Verified live via the real brew-button signal chain: resolves
  to itself at 140% quality, no regression on Pale Ale's own combo.

--- Saison -------------------------------------------------------------------
  yeast 302 (Ale) | required malt: Pilsner mallas (100)
  EBC 14-18 | IBU 16-19 | ABV 6.0%
  Real: SRM 5-14 (~EBC 10-28), IBU 20-35. Window sits inside the real
  range.
  CHANGED: added required_malt_id = Pilsner malt — realistic (Saison is
  classically brewed on a pale Pilsner base) and fixes a real bug: Saison's
  own minimum combo was resolving to "Pale Ale" for the same reason as
  Witbier above (no malt requirement previously, alphabetically after
  pale_ale.tres). Verified fixed.

--- Belgian Dubbel -------------------------------------------------------------
  yeast 302 (Ale) | min 4kg malt | EBC 31-35 | IBU 15-19 | ABV 7.0%
  Real: SRM 10-17 (~EBC 20-33) though often brewed darker with dark candi
  sugar (effectively higher), IBU 15-25, ABV 6-7.6%. Reasonable match.

--- Belgian Strong Dark Ale -----------------------------------------------------
  yeast 302 (Ale) | min 4kg malt | EBC 36-40 | IBU 20-24 | ABV 9.0%
  Real: SRM 12-22 nominal but commonly much darker with candi sugar/dark
  malts in practice, IBU 20-35, ABV 8-12%. In range.

--- Scotch Ale / Wee Heavy --------------------------------------------------
  yeast 302 (Ale) | min 4kg malt | EBC 41-55 | IBU 15-25 | ABV 7.5%
  Real: SRM 17-25 (~EBC 33-49), IBU 17-35, ABV 6.5-10%. Close match.

--- Barleywine (American/English Barleywine) --------------------------------
  yeast 302 (Ale) | min 5kg malt | EBC 56-70 | IBU 35-60 | ABV 10.0%
  Real: SRM 8-22 (English) to 14-22 (American) nominal (~EBC 16-44), but
  real barleywines often read darker in the glass than their SRM suggests
  due to intense malt concentration; IBU 35-70 (American) matches well.
  min_malt_weight=5 reflects the very high OG.

--- Imperial Stout (Russian Imperial Stout) ----------------------------------
  yeast 302 (Ale) | min 5kg malt | EBC 96-140 | IBU 45-75 | ABV 9.5%
  Real: SRM 30-40+ (effectively opaque/off-the-chart, ~EBC 60+), IBU
  50-90. Matches the "as dark as this game's malt data goes" ceiling
  (Tumma karamelli malt tops out at EBC 140) and IBU range is realistic.

--- Alkoholiton IPA (non-alcoholic IPA) --------------------------------------
  yeast 303 | min 4kg malt | EBC 12-28 | IBU 40-200 | ABV 0.3%
  Same EBC/IBU shape as regular IPA, just the alcohol-free yeast — correct
  approach (modern NA IPAs target full IPA hop character at near-zero ABV).

--- Alkoholiton Lager (non-alcoholic lager) ----------------------------------
  yeast 304 | min 3kg malt | EBC 11-14 | IBU 14-20 | ABV 0.3%
  Same EBC/IBU shape as Helles, non-alcoholic yeast — same reasoning as
  above, matches real NA lager conventions (usually a Helles-style base).


===============================================================================
DUPLICATE / AMBIGUITY CHECK
===============================================================================
Verified live (game_eval) after every change: looped all 22 styles through
compute_minimum_ingredients() -> resolve_brew_style() and confirmed every
single one resolves back to itself, with zero ambiguous matches.

Two real collisions were found and fixed during this pass:
  1. Witbier's own minimum recipe was resolving to "Pale Ale".
  2. Saison's own minimum recipe was resolving to "Pale Ale".
Both caused by Pale Ale's wide, malt-agnostic window plus alphabetical
file-load order beating them to the match. Fixed structurally (required-
malt styles now always checked first) rather than by narrowing Pale Ale's
already-tight, bug-tested window.

Session Ale's unrealistic paleness (flagged above as a known gap) was
fixed in a follow-up pass: given required_malt_id = Karamelli mallas and
raised to EBC 6-12, verified with zero new collisions across all 22
styles and confirmed live through the real brew-button flow.
