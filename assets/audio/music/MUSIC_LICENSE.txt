Original music, in custom/:
Composed for Pimeä Panimo by VelluVu in the Furnace tracker (Sega Genesis sound), 2026.
The Furnace project and raw export are kept in dev/music/; dev/tools/music/prepare_track.py
builds the playlist file.
custom/dark_reggae_pub.ogg                   "Dark Reggae Pub" (intro, three loops, faded ending)

AI-generated music, in christmas/ (played only in the Christmas season):
The melody is "Stille Nacht" / "Jouluyö, juhlayö" by Franz Xaver Gruber, 1818 (public domain).
For each track a plain chiptune-metal guide arrangement of the carol was written in code
for Pimeä Panimo, then re-performed with ACE-Step 1.5 (an open music model, MIT licence,
commercial use allowed, run locally) in its "cover" mode from a text description of the
style. Picked by VelluVu, 2026; levelled and faded with dev/tools/music/prepare_track.py.
christmas/jouluyo_doom.ogg                   "Jouluyö" doom metal version
christmas/jouluyo_folkmetal.ogg              "Jouluyö" Nordic folk metal version
christmas/jouluyo_chipmetal.ogg              "Jouluyö" 16-bit chiptune metal version
