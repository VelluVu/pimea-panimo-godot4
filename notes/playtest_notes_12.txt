TESTING METHODOLOGY NOTES (not gameplay bugs, worth recording)
=========================================================================
- Reconfirmed the standing game_eval fragility finding: an eval that
  crammed a for-loop + nested for-loop + await into one semicolon-
  chained line (trying to trace an LvvAgent's position/animation
  frame-by-frame across the raid sequence) hit EVAL_HUNG after the 8s
  timeout, and the session came back in game_status.status="break"
  ("Invalid access to property or key 'process_frame' on a base
  object of type 'null instance'") — get_tree() apparently went null
  mid-loop, likely a scoping artifact of cramming that much control
  flow onto one line rather than a real engine bug. Confirmed (again)
  that resume() does not recover from this — only
  project_manage(op="stop") + project_run again gets the session back.
  Should have used game_manage(get_node_info) polled from separate
  tool calls instead of an in-eval loop, exactly like this session's
  earlier successful single-shot position checks did.
- Confirmed project_manage(op="stop")'s "kill, not close_requested"
  behavior from playtest_notes_11 protects against exactly this kind
  of crash: reloaded the player's real save after the hang and every
  stat (276.5 €, 83 maine, day 4, risk 2%, 1 batch) was byte-for-byte
  the same as before the hang and before the in-session LVV-raid test
  that had confiscated that batch and dropped money/reputation — none
  of that session's live mutations touched the actual save file.
- Two back-to-back forced group_event spawns (zgen_lauma, then
  sekalauma before the first had fully resolved) is not a realistic
  play scenario but didn't produce any errors or crossed-wire dialogue
  bubbles beyond the expected visual overlap of two unrelated shared-
  slot bubbles rendering at once — CustomerManager's per-slot dialogue
  bookkeeping held up fine under that stress.


CONFIRMED WORKING — SCREEN-OVERFLOW FIXES (this session's earlier work)
=========================================================================
- Group chant clamp: forcing zgen_lauma's group event while the crowd
  was still on the stairs (x≈4, the left screen edge) showed the
  chant bubble ("SIGMA SIGMA BOY, EI CAP, TÄÄ ON W-KUPPILA FR FR!")
  rendered fully on-screen, its left edge flush with x=0 instead of
  bleeding into negative space — exactly the dialog_view.gd
  _place_bubble() clamp doing its job on the one path (group chants
  near the stairs) that used to expose the bug most reliably.
- Banner dynamic height: forcing sekalauma (whose banner_text is one
  112-character unbroken line, previously identified as one of the
  two group events that actually overflowed the old fixed-100px
  banner box) showed the banner correctly word-wrapped across multiple
  lines with no clipping top or bottom, confirming
  GUI._fit_banner_height()'s live remeasure-and-resize is working.
- TooltipFactory: called make_wrapped_tooltip() directly for both a
  long test string and a short one ("3 kg"). Long text correctly
  turns on autowrap (mode 3/WORD_SMART) and caps custom_minimum_size.x
  at 260; short text stays autowrap-off with no forced minimum width
  — confirms the "only clamp when actually needed" behavior intended
  for IngredientOptionButton/BarContactOptionButton/etc.'s tooltips.

VERDICT
==========
Every fix verified in this session's earlier work held up under an
independent live pass on the player's real save: bubble/chant clamp,
banner dynamic height, and tooltip wrapping all behaved exactly as
designed against the specific inputs that used to break them. The
LVV-raid squad's walk-in/pickup/walk-out/recap-timing sequence ran

game_eval line — a crash here costs a full stop/relaunch cycle for no
gameplay reason. No regressions found anywhere else touched this
session. Automated suite held at 106/106 throughout.
